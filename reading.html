The shelf, in reading order

    Weekly rhythm

    • Leadership books: 20–30 minutes on 4–5 evenings a week. Audiobooks count, and they fit drives to the lake.
    • Technical books: read on weeknights, do the hands-on part in your Windows Server lab on the weekend.
    • Notes: one page per book in the Leuchtturm. Three takeaways and one thing you'll actually do.
    • Speaking tie-in: when you finish a book, record a 3-minute summary. It doubles as Commanding Presence practice and gives you interview stories.
    • Behind? Skip ahead rather than stall. The handbook and the audit manual are reference books, so skimming chapters you know is fine.
